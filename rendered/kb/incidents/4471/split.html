Intake's tag is wrong for 43 of these 412 reports: it is guessed from the reporter's browser, not the tenant, and it has no tag for a one-day move.

The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so the day and month swap. This is the fix that is queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date day first. People type what the hint says. The parser fix does not reach these.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a month. Lisbon gets Tuesday, Toronto gets Monday. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json; the cause table still files them under parser.

How they were counted

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports across its pages.
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant (locale and booking form, causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and an en-US browser in Lisbon is still Lisbon.
  • The desk’s one-day moves are taken out first, because no date in them is swapped.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line is not a matching cause (decision 0001). “Wrong day on the invite” covers all three problems.
  • Read a sample of the bodies before marking a big ticket known.
